## Appendix: Data Sources and Methodology

### ROI Calculation Methodology

**Conservative ROI Estimate: 150-250% in First Year**

**Assumptions:**
- Average workshop material cost: $50,000/month
- Material waste reduction: 15% (conservative, not maximum 20%)
- Monthly material savings: $7,500
- Annual material savings: $90,000
- Software cost: $3,000-4,000/month ($36,000-48,000/year)
- Time savings value: $2,000-3,000/month (labor cost savings)
- Annual time savings: $24,000-36,000
- **Total annual savings: $114,000-126,000**
- **ROI: 237-350%** (using $36,000 software cost)
- **Conservative estimate: 150-250%** (accounting for implementation time, learning curve, and variability)

**Data Sources:**
- Pilot workshop data (5 workshops, 6-month period)
- Industry benchmarks for material waste (25-30% baseline)
- Time-motion studies (3 workshops)
- CNC machine output validation (accuracy measurements)

### Market Size Methodology

**5,000 Addressable Workshops:**
- Based on industry research and market analysis
- Includes: 3,000 aluminum workshops, 2,000 UPVC factories
- Geographic distribution: Egypt (3,500), Middle East (1,500)
- Market research sources: Industry associations, trade publications, field surveys

### Growth Projections Methodology

**Conservative Adoption Rates:**
- Year 1: 10-15% of addressable market (500-750 workshops)
- Year 2: 30-40% of addressable market (1,500-2,000 workshops)
- Year 3: 60-80% of addressable market (3,000-4,000 workshops)
- Based on typical B2B SaaS adoption curves in traditional industries
