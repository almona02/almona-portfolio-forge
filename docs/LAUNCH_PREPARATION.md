## 📱 User Onboarding Flow

### Step 1: Welcome to AI-Powered Fabrication

**Message:**
"Your system now learns and improves with every job. Watch as optimization gets faster and more accurate over time."

**Actions:**
- Show ML prediction in action
- Demonstrate pre-solver speed
- Highlight automatic learning

### Step 2: Meet Your Calibration Wizard

**Message:**
"Fine-tune cuts for your specific workshop conditions. The system learns from your adjustments and suggests optimal settings."

**Actions:**
- Open CalibrationWizard
- Walk through calibration process
- Show test result recording

### Step 3: Discover the Remnant Marketplace

**Message:**
"Buy low-cost materials and sell your excess stock. Turn waste into revenue across the fabricator network."

**Actions:**
- Browse marketplace listings
- Create a test listing
- Show purchase flow

### Step 4: Track Your Performance

**Message:**
"Real-time insights into your workshop efficiency. See OEE, waste reduction, and cost savings at a glance."

**Actions:**
- Show performance dashboard
- Explain metrics
- Highlight trends
