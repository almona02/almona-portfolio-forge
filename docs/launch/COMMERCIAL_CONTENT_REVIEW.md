# Commercial information and public claims

19 September 2026 — items 2 and 4 of the public-site launch review.

## Release decision

The public site now uses an enquiry-first approach wherever approved commercial records were unavailable. No new prices, discounts, stock quantities, delivery deadlines, customer outcomes or institutional endorsements were invented. These changes are local and have not been deployed.

| Area | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service plans | Conflicting prices; unsubstantiated discounts and response commitments | Quote-only plans with scope and timing agreed in writing |
| Service calculator | Arbitrary confidence and savings percentages; prices without approval | Machine-count guide, no money or savings calculation; explicit enquiry action |
| Service landing page | Static satisfaction, response, customer totals and testimonials | Replaced with plan comparison and contact routes; advanced sample dashboard is signed-in only and labelled demonstration |
| Machine catalogue and shop | Internal static price list presented as current offers | Public cards and enquiry adapters no longer advertise those amounts; internal pricing records preserved |
| Delivery, warranty, certificates | Blanket free shipping, one-year warranty, 2–4 week delivery and certification badges | Request model-specific written terms and conformity documents |
| Training | Rolling dates calculated from today's date; unapproved fees and credential claims | No generated cohorts; request fees, dates and completion-document details; enquiries do not reserve a place |
| Used machines | Three static listings, missing photographs, seller ratings and verification flags without supporting records | Listings remain unpublished; draft data retained for review; removed automatic stock/rating assertions from search metadata |
| Home page | Claimed import savings, trainee/customer totals and carbon reductions without supporting measurements | Replaced outcome counters with practical service links |
| Digital Egypt | Named prospective sponsors and partners, numeric pilot targets mixed with achieved capabilities | Rewritten as an independent proposal, with no claimed sponsorship, partnership, funding or results |
| Product marketing | Blanket uptime, savings, IoT, digital-twin, blockchain and certification claims | Model-specific information requests; unsupported aggregate rating/stock search data removed |
| Prestige Agent | Static connected/online labels, component count and response speed | Interface labels no longer claim measured connection health or a fixed response time |

## Evidence reviewed and limits

- Repository source showed the rolling cohort generator, hard-coded marketing metrics and sample service telemetry. These are implementation evidence, not business performance evidence.
- The [official YILMAZ site](https://www.yilmazmachine.com.tr/en/) describes its machinery and manufacturer operations. It does not establish ALMONA's prices, response times, customer counts, environmental results or individual machine conformity. Manufacturer-wide statements were not copied into ALMONA-specific claims.
- Search results for similarly named ALMONA businesses were not treated as evidence about this company.
- The company history and dealer relationship already supplied by ALMONA's project are retained as owner-provided background, not independently verified facts. Keep registration/history and current dealership documentation available before treating them as verified marketing claims.
- No approved price sheet, stock ledger, warranty schedule, enrolment calendar, seller authorisation, partnership agreement or measured impact report was supplied. The absence of these records does not prove the underlying claim false; it means the website should not present it as verified.
- This pass covers the public surfaces identified in the launch review. It is not an audit of every machine technical specification, protected application, document download, language string or historical source file. Fabricator remains outside scope.

## Evidence required before restoring claims

| Claim | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Price or discount | Approved price sheet, currency, tax treatment, scope, effective date and expiry |
| Stock or lead time | Current SKU/serial record and supplier confirmation with a check date |
| Warranty or response SLA | Written terms stating model, region, exclusions and responsible provider |
| Training dates and credentials | Confirmed venue/instructor/capacity/fees; issuer and nature of any completion document |
| Used-machine listing | Seller permission, actual photographs, serial/model, condition inspection, location and asking price |
| Certification | Applicable manufacturer's declaration/certificate for the exact model/configuration; no blanket catalogue badge |
| Customer totals or savings | Dated measurement/report, methodology and permission for identifiable testimonials |
| Government or institutional relationship | Signed or publicly announced agreement defining the relationship |
