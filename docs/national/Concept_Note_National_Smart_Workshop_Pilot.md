# CONCEPT NOTE: National "Smart Workshop" Pilot Program

**To:** Ministry of Communications & Information Technology (MCIT)  
**From:** Almona Industrial Solutions  
**Subject:** Supporting the "Digital Egypt" Strategy for Industrial SMEs  

## 1. Executive Summary

The industrial SME sector represents a significant opportunity for digitalization. Currently, material waste in this sector contributes to unnecessary import demand. Almona respectfully proposes a private-sector-led pilot to digitize 10-15 workshops using the Fabricator Pro platform, aiming to validate a model for national industrial modernization.

## 2. Alignment with National Goals

*   **Economic:** 15-30% reduction in raw material waste (Import Substitution).
*   **Social:** Certification of 50 youth as "Digital Fabricators."
*   **Strategic:** Creation of a sovereign data layer for manufacturing intelligence.

## 3. The "Servant" Model

Almona seeks no government funding for this pilot. We operate as a "Servant of the Vision," providing:
1.  **Free Software Licenses** for pilot workshops.
2.  **Technical Training** for workshop staff.
3.  **Data Integration** with national indicators.

## 4. Expected Outcomes (90 Days)

*   **Data Sovereignty:** 100% of pilot data hosted on Sovereign Cloud (Egypt-resident).
*   **Formalization:** Onboarding of informal workshops into the digital economy.
*   **Sustainability:** Measurable reduction in aluminium scrap generation.

## 5. Next Steps

We respectfully request a technical meeting to demonstrate the "National Manufacturing Dashboard" and discuss the selection criteria for pilot workshops.
