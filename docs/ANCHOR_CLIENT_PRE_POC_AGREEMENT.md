## Objective

To collaboratively validate whether ALMONA's core pipeline (BIM → Geometry → Cut List) can deliver ≥40% time savings in the Client's real production environment using a Minimal Viable Pipeline (MVP).

**Core Hypothesis:** ALMONA can extract geometry from BIM files, generate accurate cut lists, and reduce design-to-cut-list time by ≥40% compared to current process.

## Success Metric: Go/No-Go Gate

### Go Criteria (Proceed to Full Anchor Client POC)

**If validation shows ≥40% time savings in "Design → Cut List" process:**

- ✅ Both parties proceed to negotiate full Anchor Client POC agreement for Phase 1
- ✅ Client becomes anchor client with first-mover pricing
- ✅ ALMONA incorporates validated learnings into official Phase 1 plan
- ✅ 48-week roadmap begins with de-risked, client-validated plan

### No-Go Criteria (Re-scope Phase 1)

**If validation shows <40% time savings:**

- ❌ ALMONA must re-scope Phase 1 fundamentally
- ❌ Client has no obligation to proceed
- ❌ ALMONA provides diagnostic analysis and recommendations
- ❌ Either party can exit with 1 week notice

**Acceptable No-Go Reasons:**

- Time savings <40% (core hypothesis invalidated)
- MVP cannot extract geometry accurately (>2% variance)
- Workflow takes longer than current process
- Critical feature missing for Client's projects

**Unacceptable No-Go Reasons:**

- "Wanted full ERP integration" (out of scope)
- "Expected structural analysis" (constitutionally prohibited)
- "UI needs complete redesign" (aesthetic preference)

## Key Clauses

### 1. No Commercial Obligation

This agreement does **not** obligate Client to purchase ALMONA. Client can exit at any time with 1 week notice if core hypothesis is invalidated.

### 2. Data Ownership

- **Client retains:** All project data (BIM files, designs, cut lists)
- **ALMONA may use:** Aggregated, anonymized results for case studies (with permission)
- **ALMONA may use:** Feedback for product improvement (becomes ALMONA IP)

### 3. Exit Clause

Either party may exit with **1 week notice** if:
- Core hypothesis is invalidated (<40% time savings)
- MVP cannot meet basic requirements (geometry extraction fails)
- Client cannot provide required resources (projects, technical lead)

### 4. Confidentiality

- Both parties maintain confidentiality of proprietary information
- Client data encrypted in transit and at rest
- ALMONA system architecture remains confidential

### 5. Intellectual Property

- **ALMONA retains:** All IP in ALMONA system and MVP
- **Client retains:** All IP in Client's designs and projects
- **Feedback becomes:** ALMONA property (for product improvement)
