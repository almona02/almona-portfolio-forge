## Presentation Delivery Notes

### Opening (5 minutes)

- Start with the problem (Slide 2) - make it relatable
- Emphasize transparency and legal clarity (differentiation)
- Position as partnership, not vendor relationship

### Middle (20 minutes)

- Focus on what ALMONA offers (Slide 3) - concrete, not abstract
- Explain acceptance criteria clearly (Slide 6) - measurable, not vague
- Address scope boundaries explicitly (Slide 7) - prevents disappointment

### Closing (10 minutes)

- Emphasize mutual benefit (Slide 11) - partnership, not one-way
- Make the ask clear (Slide 12) - specific commitments, timeline
- Leave time for Q&A (Slide 13) - address concerns immediately

### Key Messages to Reinforce

1. **"We calculate, you decide"** - Authority stays with you
2. **"Transparent, not black-box"** - All calculations traceable
3. **"Partnership, not vendor"** - Your feedback shapes development
4. **"Reality check, not feature list"** - We validate what works
