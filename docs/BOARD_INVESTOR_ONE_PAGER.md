## What ALMONA Is

**ALMONA is a governed industrial computing platform for fabrication—not engineering software—designed for national-scale deployment in Egypt and MENA.**

We translate architectural intent (BIM) into fabrication reality (cut lists, CNC, BOM) with explicit authority boundaries, full audit trails, and institutional-grade governance.

---

## The Market Problem

**Legacy software (Orgadata/Klaes) mixes calculation, judgment, and liability:**
- Black-box algorithms with limited auditability
- Ambiguous authority boundaries (who's responsible?)
- Proprietary formats create vendor lock-in
- Not designed for national/multi-tenant deployment

**Enterprises & governments now require:**
- Full auditability and governance
- Explicit authority separation
- Legal clarity and defensibility
- National-scale architecture

**Workshops still need:**
- Deterministic execution (99.8%+ accuracy)
- Machine-ready outputs (CNC, cut lists, BOM)
- Transparent cost calculations
- No black-box surprises

## Risk & Success Probability

### Success Probability Assessment

| Scenario | Probability | Key Factors |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| **With discipline + anchor client** | **80-85%** | Constitutional boundaries enforced, real-world validation |
| **With discipline, no anchor client** | **80-85%** | Governance model strong, but slower market validation |
| **Without discipline (feature creep)** | **<25%** | Authority boundaries collapse, legal risk increases |

### Why Risk Is Controlled

**1. Constitutional Guardian Infrastructure (Phase 0)**
- CI/CD enforces boundaries (can't merge prohibited code)
- Dashboard monitors institutional health
- Tests validate constitutional compliance

**2. Anchor Client Gate (Week 16)**
- Hard gate: 3 real projects must pass
- Prevents building "beautiful but unused" pipeline
- Forces market reality check before Phase 2 complexity

**3. Human Responsibility Hooks**
- All pricing decisions require explicit human approval
- Immutable audit trail (RealityOS) proves responsibility
- Legal liability stays with humans, not system

**4. Explicit Scope Limitations**
- NOT IN SCOPE 2026 document (constitutional guardrail)
- No structural analysis, no engineering replacement
- Clear boundaries prevent feature creep

### Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Authority Overreach** | Constitutional Guardian + CI/CD enforcement |
| **Market Timing** | Anchor client gate validates demand before Phase 2 |
| **Technical Complexity** | 30% Phase 1 scope reduction, authority-first architecture |
| **Adoption Risk** | Legacy project reference mode, coexistence model |

### Explicit Kill Condition

**If Anchor Client Acceptance (Week 16) fails twice for the same core reason (geometry accuracy, workflow speed, or CNC compatibility), Phase 2 is paused and scope is reduced before further capital deployment.**

This signals capital discipline, reduces fear of sunk-cost continuation, and protects investor capital.
