## 2. Liability & Responsibility Framework

### 2.1 ALMONA's Responsibilities

**What ALMONA Provides:**

- ✅ BIM import capability (Revit/ArchiCAD)
- ✅ Curtain wall system engine (unitized/stick systems)
- ✅ BOM and cut list generation (deterministic, from BOM truth)
- ✅ CNC export capability (machine-ready files)
- ✅ Technical support during POC period (12 weeks)

**What ALMONA Does NOT Provide:**

- ❌ Structural analysis or engineering judgment
- ❌ Pricing decisions or business recommendations
- ❌ ERP integration or automated purchasing
- ❌ Guarantees beyond acceptance criteria thresholds

**Liability Limits:**

- ALMONA's liability is limited to **correcting defects** that prevent meeting acceptance criteria
- ALMONA is **not liable** for:
  - Business decisions made using ALMONA outputs
  - Engineering judgments based on ALMONA calculations
  - Material waste or cost overruns (unless caused by ALMONA calculation errors >0.5% variance)
  - Project delays (unless caused by ALMONA system failures)

### 2.2 Client's Responsibilities

**What Client Provides:**

- ✅ 3 real projects with contractual deadlines
- ✅ Senior technical lead (8+ hours/week)
- ✅ Revit files and system pack requirements
- ✅ Time-motion study data (current vs ALMONA process)
- ✅ Honest feedback and acceptance certificate (if criteria met)

**What Client Does NOT Provide:**

- ❌ Payment for Phase 1 POC (free access)
- ❌ Guarantee of acceptance (validation is objective)
- ❌ Exclusive commitment (client can use other systems)

**Client Liability:**

- Client is responsible for:
  - Engineering decisions (structural analysis, code compliance)
  - Pricing decisions (margins, discounts, final prices)
  - Project delivery (deadlines, quality, client satisfaction)
  - Material procurement (ordering, inventory, waste management)

### 2.3 Shared Responsibilities

**Both Parties:**

- ✅ Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information
- ✅ Provide honest feedback and accurate data
- ✅ Respect constitutional boundaries (no structural analysis requests)
- ✅ Follow acceptance criteria objectively (not subjective preferences)

## 3. Intellectual Property Rights

### 3.1 ALMONA's IP

**ALMONA Retains:**

- ✅ All intellectual property in ALMONA system
- ✅ Right to use client feedback for product improvement
- ✅ Right to reference client as "anchor client" (with permission)
- ✅ Right to use anonymized data for case studies

**ALMONA Grants Client:**

- ✅ License to use ALMONA system during POC period (12 weeks)
- ✅ License to use ALMONA outputs (cut lists, BOMs, CNC files) for client projects
- ✅ Right to provide feedback and suggestions

### 3.2 Client's IP

**Client Retains:**

- ✅ All intellectual property in client's BIM files, designs, and projects
- ✅ Right to use ALMONA outputs for client's commercial projects
- ✅ Right to terminate POC if acceptance criteria not met

**Client Grants ALMONA:**

- ✅ Right to use anonymized project data for system improvement
- ✅ Right to reference client as "anchor client" (with written permission)
- ✅ Right to use feedback for product development

### 3.3 Feedback & Improvements

**Client Feedback:**

- Client feedback becomes ALMONA property (for product improvement)
- Client retains no ownership of improvements made based on feedback
- Client benefits from improvements (as part of Phase 2 beta program)

**ALMONA Improvements:**

- ALMONA retains all IP in improvements made during POC
- Client receives benefit of improvements (as part of POC)
- No additional payment required for improvements

## 4. Acceptance Criteria Legal Framework

### 4.1 Objective vs Subjective Criteria

**Objective Criteria (Binding):**

- Geometry accuracy: ≤0.5% variance (measurable, database comparison)
- Time savings: ≥40% reduction (measurable, time-motion study)
- CNC integration: Files load without errors (testable, binary outcome)
- Constitutional compliance: 100% (testable, CI/CD validation)

**Subjective Criteria (Non-Binding):**

- User satisfaction: ≥4/5 (survey-based, but not blocking)
- UI preferences: Not part of acceptance criteria
- Feature requests: Not part of acceptance criteria

### 4.2 Acceptance Certificate Legal Effect

**If Acceptance Certificate Signed:**

- ✅ Phase 1 considered complete (ALMONA side)
- ✅ Client commits to Phase 2 beta participation (optional, but expected)
- ✅ Client can serve as reference client (with permission)
- ✅ ALMONA can proceed to Phase 2 development

**If Acceptance Certificate NOT Signed:**

- ❌ Phase 1 not considered complete (ALMONA side)
- ❌ ALMONA must address rejection reasons (Week 17-22)
- ❌ Client has no obligation to continue
- ❌ ALMONA cannot proceed to Phase 2 (until acceptance or scope reduction)

### 4.3 Dispute Resolution

**If Criteria Disputed:**

1. **Technical Disputes:** Resolved by independent technical auditor (mutually agreed)
2. **Business Disputes:** Resolved by mediation (before arbitration/litigation)
3. **Constitutional Disputes:** Resolved by ALMONA Constitutional Guardian (final authority)

**Acceptable Dispute Reasons:**

- Measurement methodology disagreement (how to measure accuracy)
- Interpretation of acceptance criteria (what "matches" means)
- System failure preventing validation (ALMONA system errors)

**Unacceptable Dispute Reasons:**

- Subjective preferences (UI design, feature requests)
- Out-of-scope requirements (ERP integration, structural analysis)
- Business model disagreements (pricing, margins)

## 5. Confidentiality & Data Protection

### 5.1 Confidential Information

**ALMONA Confidential:**

- System architecture and algorithms
- Constitutional boundaries and governance model
- Development roadmap and timelines
- Pricing and commercial terms

**Client Confidential:**

- BIM files and project designs
- Business processes and workflows
- Material costs and pricing strategies
- Client relationships and contracts

### 5.2 Data Protection

**ALMONA Responsibilities:**

- ✅ Encrypt all client data in transit and at rest
- ✅ Limit access to client data (only POC team)
- ✅ Delete client data after POC (unless retention required for support)
- ✅ Comply with data protection regulations (GDPR, local laws)

**Client Responsibilities:**

- ✅ Provide accurate data (not falsified or manipulated)
- ✅ Not share ALMONA system access with unauthorized parties
- ✅ Report security incidents immediately

### 5.3 Data Retention

**During POC:**

- Client data retained for POC duration (12 weeks)
- ALMONA can use anonymized data for system improvement

**After POC:**

- If accepted: Client data retained for Phase 2 beta (with permission)
- If rejected: Client data deleted within 30 days (unless retention required)
- Anonymized data may be retained for case studies (with permission)

## 6. Termination & Exit

### 6.1 Termination by Client

**Client Can Terminate:**

- ✅ If acceptance criteria not met (after Week 16 evaluation)
- ✅ If ALMONA fails to provide promised capabilities
- ✅ If constitutional boundaries violated (structural analysis attempted)
- ✅ For convenience (with 2 weeks notice, no penalty)

**Client Obligations on Termination:**

- Return all ALMONA proprietary materials
- Delete ALMONA system access
- Provide exit feedback (what worked, what didn't)

### 6.2 Termination by ALMONA

**ALMONA Can Terminate:**

- ✅ If client fails to provide required resources (technical lead, projects)
- ✅ If client violates confidentiality (shares system access)
- ✅ If client requests constitutionally prohibited features (structural analysis)
- ✅ For convenience (with 2 weeks notice, no penalty)

**ALMONA Obligations on Termination:**

- Provide exit data export (client's project data)
- Delete client data (unless retention required)
- Provide exit feedback (what worked, what didn't)

### 6.3 Exit Data

**Client Data Export:**

- ALMONA provides export of client's project data (BIM files, cut lists, BOMs)
- Format: Standard formats (CSV, DXF, PDF)
- Timeline: Within 30 days of termination

**ALMONA Data Retention:**

- ALMONA may retain anonymized data for case studies (with permission)
- ALMONA may retain feedback for product improvement (becomes ALMONA IP)

## 8. Dispute Resolution

### 8.1 Escalation Path

**Level 1: Technical Discussion**
- ALMONA technical lead + Client technical lead
- Resolve technical disputes (accuracy, workflow, features)

**Level 2: Business Discussion**
- ALMONA business lead + Client business lead
- Resolve business disputes (scope, timeline, commitments)

**Level 3: Executive Discussion**
- ALMONA executive + Client executive
- Resolve strategic disputes (partnership, reference client status)

**Level 4: Mediation**
- Independent mediator (mutually agreed)
- Resolve legal disputes (liability, IP, termination)

**Level 5: Arbitration/Litigation**
- Final dispute resolution (if mediation fails)
- Governed by jurisdiction and governing law

### 8.2 Constitutional Disputes

**If Constitutional Boundary Disputed:**

- ALMONA Constitutional Guardian has final authority
- Constitutional boundaries are non-negotiable (NOT IN SCOPE 2026)
- Client cannot request constitutionally prohibited features
- Dispute resolution: Constitutional Guardian decision is final
