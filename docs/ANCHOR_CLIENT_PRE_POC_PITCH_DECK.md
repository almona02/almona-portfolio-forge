## Presentation Delivery Notes

### Opening (5 minutes)

- Start with the problem (Slide 2) - make it relatable
- Position as validation partnership, not product demo
- Emphasize risk-free nature (no commercial obligation)

### Middle (15 minutes)

- Explain the hypothesis clearly (Slide 3) - what we're testing
- Show the MVP scope (Slide 5) - minimal, focused
- Explain measurement framework (Slide 8) - objective proof
- Address commitment clearly (Slide 9) - specific, not vague

### Closing (5 minutes)

- Emphasize mutual benefit (Slide 11) - partnership, not one-way
- Address risk mitigation (Slide 12) - you're protected
- Make the ask clear (Slide 13) - specific commitments, timeline
- Leave time for Q&A (Slide 14) - address concerns immediately

### Key Messages to Reinforce

1. **"We're validating, not selling"** - This is a test, not a commitment
2. **"You're protected"** - No commercial obligation, can exit anytime
3. **"Objective proof"** - Time-motion study, not subjective claims
4. **"Partnership"** - Your feedback shapes development
