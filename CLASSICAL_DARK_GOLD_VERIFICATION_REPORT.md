## 🎯 Classical Aesthetic Elements

### ✅ Applied Elements

1. **Dark Textured Background**
   - Base: `#0a0a0a` (deep black)
   - Overlay: `#0f0f0f`, `#1a1a1a` (layered depth)
   - Texture: Radial gradients + repeating linear patterns
   - Opacity: 10-30% for subtle classical feel

2. **Gold/Amber Color Scheme**
   - Primary: `amber-200` to `amber-300` (text)
   - Secondary: `amber-400` to `amber-500` (accents)
   - Tertiary: `amber-600/70` (subtle text)
   - Borders: `amber-600/30` to `amber-600/50`

3. **Thick Borders**
   - All borders: `border-2` (2px)
   - Amber tones throughout
   - Creates formal, architectural feel

4. **Glow Effects**
   - Drop shadows with amber: `drop-shadow-[0_0_*px_rgba(245,158,11,*)]`
   - Box shadows: `shadow-[0_0_*px_rgba(245,158,11,*)]`
   - Creates depth and premium feel

5. **Typography**
   - Bold/semibold weights
   - Tracking for uppercase text
   - Amber colors throughout
   - Formal, authoritative feel

6. **Architectural Elements**
   - Ornate border frames
   - Side pillars (gradient borders)
   - Corner accents (L-shaped)
   - Decorative horizontal bars
   - Classical pediments
